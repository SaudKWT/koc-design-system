What happened
  • OD1 was idle for a second week, still waiting on BOUM-0001 location clearance to start jack-down. Last week activities are again recorded as Nil.
  • Overstay crew fell from 11 to 8. COSL came down from 11 to 7, and HLB now holds 1 where it held none.
  • OD1 personnel onboard eased from 79 to 78, and visa holders rose from 57 to 58.
  • CA1 took 300 m³ of fuel from the bunker barge MT SEDRA-II at Mina Al Ahmadi anchorage on Sat 12. The workbook records it as an adjustment to balance the 294.2 m³ given to OPH on 06 and 07 Sep, which settles where last week's fuel went.
  • Vessel movements were 23 against 18, the busiest of the four weeks tracked. Charlie-3 made 13 of them.
  • CA5 has now spent 14 straight days on station at OD1 without moving between locations, across both this week and last.
  • Truck and pickup moves held at 44, but turned outbound again: 25 out against 19 in, where the week before ran 12 out and 32 in as the BM-0001 casing arrived.
  • Casing went back out: 30", 24" and 36" and 38" R3 joints returned to KOC Ahmadi Yard 4 across Sat 12, Sun 13 and Mon 14, and 18-5/8" R3 went to HETCO for inspection and came back to the Shuaiba pipe yard.
  • Charlie-3 ran crew changes through two new transfer points, Jal Boot Village and Khiran Marina, alongside the usual Shuaiba runs.
  • Vessel movements remain under MARSEC Level 2.
