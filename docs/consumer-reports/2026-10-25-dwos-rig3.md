# DWOS takes the rig3 language — measured in the built app

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Registry:** `9aec460` (branch `claude/rig3-design-language`,
untagged) · **Consumer:** dwos-platform `008e3a2` (branch `rig3-redesign`) ·
**Verdict: clean take; two defects found in the consumer and fixed at the
source before the re-take; two consumer-side changes are required by design.**

Saud chose the DWEG landing's rig3 page as the base for the whole system. The
rework (tokens `fontWeight.light` + `letterSpacing.caps`; Card, StatCard,
PageHeader/SectionHeading, Sparkline; the AppShell; `@koc/chart` in plain SVG
with Recharts removed) was synced into dwos-platform and the call-off and
vessel screens recomposed on it. Everything below was measured in that app.

## What was run

1. `sync-registry.mjs 51e8d00 --from <this repo>`, then items re-added one per
   invocation with `--overwrite`: theme, org, card, sparkline, stat-card,
   page-header, chart, app-shell, filter-tabs.
2. `tsc -b` → **green with no local edits** (no importer ripple this time:
   every changed prop is additive — `tag`, `trend`, `description`, `density`,
   `eyebrow`, `brandMark`, `NavGroup.code`).
3. Recomposed the call-off overview in the rig3 page shape; found the two
   defects below; fixed them here (`9aec460`); re-synced; re-added chart and
   app-shell.
4. `vite build` green, `check-motion` green (87 files), Playwright 17/17
   against the live API.

## Defects found from outside, fixed at the source

1. **Axis labels collided.** XLabels thinned by a fixed 52px per label, and
   always drew the last label even when it crowded the one before:
   "8 Oct–14 Oct" ran into "15 Oct–21 Oct". The docs pages use short labels
   ("Jun", "03 Aug") and could not show it. Now room is sized from the
   longest label, and thinning counts back from the last category.
2. **The sidebar repeated itself.** The unit zone was labelled with the unit
   name directly under the switcher that already names it, and DWOS's single
   team-wide group ("Team-wide") sat inside a zone called "Team-wide" — a
   folded row that only repeated its label. The zone now reads
   "Applications", and a zone holding one group of its own name lists its
   screens directly.

## Required consumer changes (by design, not defects)

- **No top bar on desktop.** Anything a consumer rendered as a row above its
  pages to fill that strip should move. DWOS had a theme toggle there; it is
  now an item in the account menu (`userMenu` → `onSelect`). Pages that used
  `pt-2` because a top bar sat above them need real top padding (`pt-6`).
- **Tests that found the unit switcher by the team's name** must use its new
  accessible name (`/switch unit/i`); tests that clicked sidebar links
  directly must unfold the groups first or go through Find — the groups fold
  by default. Both DWOS specs were updated; nothing was wrong with them
  before.
- **The KOC emblem is not shipped by the registry.** `brandMark` takes a URL;
  the consumer copies `koc-emblem.svg` (white artwork, drawn in brand blue
  through a mask) into its own `public/`. Worth a `registry:file` item if a
  third consumer appears.

## Worth knowing

- `shadcn add` prefixed the re-added `avatar.tsx` with `"use client"`. Inert
  in a Vite app; noted so nobody chases the diff.
- DWOS's own `package.json` still lists `recharts`; nothing imports it any
  more. It leaves at the app's next dependency pass, which shrinks its SBOM.
- Recent records the screen being *left*, per browser — the screen you are on
  is already highlighted. Pinned survives reloads. Both are localStorage and
  degrade silently where storage is blocked.
